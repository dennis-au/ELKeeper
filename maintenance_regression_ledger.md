## Open Gates

- Do not enable host reboot, rolling restart, upgrade, evacuation, or the
  Node Shutdown backend. The release artifact currently approves none of them.
- Phase 2 reboot requires the redundant inventory-defined topology,
  controller-disconnect tests, one-shot executor packaging evidence, cleanup
  verification, and an accepted phase ledger.
- Phase 3-5 require their role-specific adapters, recovery integration,
  designated destructive-host rounds, managed-only cleanup, and phase-specific
  live acceptance before any execution control is exposed.
